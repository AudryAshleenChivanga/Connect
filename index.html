About Ashletech Connect SRHR

Bridging the knowledge gap in sexual and reproductive health for young people

Our Mission

Ashletech Connect SRHR is committed to providing young people in Sub-Saharan Africa with accurate, accessible, and confidential information about sexual and reproductive health. We believe that informed individuals make better decisions about their health and well-being.

Our Impact

  • 100+ girls impacted through SRHR organizations
  • Winner of the 2024 Girl Up Project Award
  • Partnered with Girl Up Rwanda
  • Aiming to impact 10,000+ girls and women by 2030

What We Offer

  • Confidential and safe environment
  • Evidence-based health information
  • Community support and peer education
  • Access to professional health services

Recognized Excellence

2024 Girl Up Project Award Winner, partnered with Girl Up Rwanda, and committed to empowering 10,000+ girls by 2030.
