WHY WE EXIST

Rights are only real when people can reach them.

We connect knowledge with practical support so girls and young people can make informed decisions about their bodies, education, and futures.

Our mission

We are a youth-led nonprofit committed to advancing sexual and reproductive health and rights, girls' education, and meaningful youth participation across countries in the Global South.

Accountable to the communities we serve
